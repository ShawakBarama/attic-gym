# 4-Day Goblin's Gym Band Program — EMOM Edition (Monday / Tuesday / Thursday / Friday)

Based on Pascal Dornier's Goblin's Gym upper/lower **A / B** split, rebuilt as an EMOM so it drops straight onto a Garmin watch. Upper days fall Monday/Thursday (legs fresh for Thursday diving); lower days Tuesday/Friday.

## How the EMOM works

- **One set = one 1-minute interval.** Do your reps in the first ~30–45 seconds, then rest the remainder of the minute. Start the next set when the watch beeps.
- **Target: 4 sets per exercise.**
  - **Bilateral** moves (both arms/legs at once) → **4 intervals**.
  - **Unilateral** moves (one side at a time) → **8 intervals**: 4 right, then 4 left.
- The **minute numbers run continuously**, so they map 1:1 onto the watch's interval counter.
- **Session length = total intervals:** Monday **28 min** · Tuesday **36 min** · Thursday **32 min** · Friday **40 min** (plus warm-up, not counted).
- **Wednesday mobility is paused.** Wrist work moved to Friday as strength work; pike compression now sits in Tuesday's hanging leg raise.
- **⚠️ 6 AM note:** Both lower days load the squat pattern — warm up hips, spine, and ankles thoroughly before Tuesday/Friday.

### Reps: two bands, two jobs

The **Progress by** column tells you which dial to turn when a lift gets easy:

- **Load** — add band tension, vest weight, or reduce assistance. Kept at **6–12 reps**; these are your strength drivers.
- **Reps** — hold the load, add reps, range, or slower tempo. Run at **12–20 reps**.

Form is the gate on both. If added tension costs you range or control, you've turned the wrong dial.

## Monday — Upper A · 28 intervals (28 min)

| # | Exercise | EMOM sets (minute) | Reps | Progress by | Cue |
|---|----------|--------------------|------|-------------|-----|
| 1 | Weighted dips (+10 kg vest) | 4 (1–4) | 6–12 | **Load** | Hardest move, done fresh. Controlled depth, no shoulder shrug |
| 2 | Ring inverted row (feet elevated) | 4 (5–8) | 6–12 | **Load** | Add the vest next; body as horizontal as you can hold |
| 3 | Lat prayer | 4 (9–12) | 12–20 | Reps | Straight arms, pull band down to hips |
| 4 | Low press-around | Right ×4 + Left ×4 (13–20) | 12–20 | Reps | Press down and across to the opposite hip |
| 5 | Ring triceps extension *(impossible dip progression)* | 4 (21–24) | 12–20 | Reps | Bodyweight skull-crusher on rings. Lower the rings or walk feet back to make it harder |
| 6 | Hammer curl | 4 (25–28) | 12–20 | Reps | Neutral grip, stand on the band, curl up |

**Impossible dip pathway:** ring triceps extension → forward-lean dips with slow eccentrics → band-assisted impossible dip negatives → impossible dip. Rings are kinder to the wrists than parallettes; if the front of the shoulder complains, cut the low press-around before the dips.

## Tuesday — Lower A + Shoulders · 36 intervals (36 min)

| # | Exercise | EMOM sets (minute) | Reps | Progress by | Cue |
|---|----------|--------------------|------|-------------|-----|
| 1 | Bulgarian split squat | Right ×4 + Left ×4 (1–8) | 6–12 | **Load** | Vest, band over shoulders, deficit, or tempo. Stability is the point |
| 2 | Nordic curl | 4 (9–12) | 6–12 | **Load** | Progress by leverage first. Fight the descent |
| 3 | High row | 4 (13–16) | 12–20 | Reps | Hinge, pull to upper chest, elbows high |
| 4 | Unilateral shoulder press | Right ×4 + Left ×4 (17–24) | 12–20 | Reps | Kept for the core demand as much as the shoulders |
| 5 | Lateral raise | Right ×4 + Left ×4 (25–32) | 12–20 | Reps | Light band, strict. Never load this one heavy |
| 6 | Hanging leg raise | 4 (33–36) | 12–20 | Reps | **Your compression driver** — progress toward straight legs, pelvis tucking under |

**On the pike press-up (press to handstand):** this is limited by pike compression and straight-arm strength, not pressing power — so pike push-ups alone won't get you there. The hanging leg raise now carries that job, which is why it's the compression driver rather than a filler. It'll take you partway; the full press needs dedicated compression work eventually.

## Friday — Lower B + Shoulders + Forearms · 40 intervals (40 min) — loaded squat day

| # | Exercise | EMOM sets (minute) | Reps | Progress by | Cue |
|---|----------|--------------------|------|-------------|-----|
| 1 | Band hack squat *(→ belt squat once the band box is built)* | 4 (1–4) | 6–12 | **Load** | Replaced the Zercher squat — see note below |
| 2 | Pike push-up | 4 (5–8) | 6–12 | **Load** | **Replaced the band overhead press.** Done early so shoulders are fresh |
| 3 | Ring leg curl | 4 (9–12) | 12–20 | Reps | Heels in rings, bridge up and curl in |
| 4 | Band pull-apart | 4 (13–16) | 12–20 | Reps | Arms forward, pull apart across the chest |
| 5 | Shrugs | 4 (17–20) | 12–20 | Reps | Band between legs, shrug straight up |
| 6 | Ab crunch (cable) | 4 (21–24) | 12–20 | Reps | Band from bar by head, crunch ribs down |
| 7 | Gripper crush | 4 (25–28) · both hands | 8–15 | **Load** | 100 lb for reps → 150 lb for low reps → negatives on 150 |
| 8 | Gripper hold + radial and ulnar deviation | Right ×4 + Left ×4 (29–36) | 8–12 | **Load** | Hold the gripper shut while a band resists side-to-side wrist movement |
| 9 | Wrist extension and flexion | 4 (37–40) · both hands | 12–20 | Reps | Band or manual resistance. **Pause at the end range** — that's what buys extension for handstands |

**Handstand push-up pathway:** pike push-up on the floor → feet elevated on the step ladder → hands on parallettes for a deeper range → wall-supported handstand push-up, partial then full → freestanding. Elevate the feet before adding range; that's what shifts load onto the shoulders. Check attic headroom before the wall stage.

**Why the overhead press went:** it duplicated Tuesday's unilateral shoulder press, and a bodyweight pike push-up is a better tool for the handstand goal. Tuesday's shoulder work is untouched.

**Why forearms go last:** grip work first would compromise your hold on the bands during hack squats, pull-aparts, and shrugs. Loaded end-range wrist work is deliberate — it builds strength and range in the same drill. Gripping hard also stiffens and protects the wrist joint, which is the mechanism the gripper-plus-band drill exploits.

**Why the Zercher went:** bands give least tension at the bottom of a squat and most at the top — the opposite of what the movement needs — and the elbow crook gives out before the legs do (Pascal's own workaround is an EZ curl bar, which you don't have). The hack squat is the better band fit; the belt squat off a band box is better still.

## Setting it up on your Garmin

**Option A — On-watch HIIT timer (fastest).** Start a **HIIT** activity → **HIIT Timers → EMOM**. Set **move time 1:00**, **rest 0:00**, **rounds = the day's total** (Monday 28 · Tuesday 36 · Thursday 32 · Friday 40). The watch won't show exercise names, so keep this sheet handy.

**Option B — Named workout in Garmin Connect (guided on-wrist).**
1. Add a **Repeat** step, count **4**, containing one work step of **Duration 1:00**; name it after the exercise.
2. For **unilateral** moves, add **two** repeat blocks — e.g. "Low press-around – Right" ×4, then "Low press-around – Left" ×4.
3. Order to match the day's table, save, sync.

## Equipment notes

- **Band box / foot plate — recommended.** A foot plate lets you load heavily without your ankles bending sideways, which is the current ceiling on your lower-body work. Unlocks belt squats, trap-bar-style deadlifts, and heavy Romanian deadlifts. A commercial plate is around $349; sturdy plywood with standoffs does the job. **Round every corner the bands run over, and never use a metal plate** — it shreds bands. In an attic, consider joist loading and noise.
- **Grippers:** 100 lb, 150 lb, 200 lb. The 200 lb is a long-term target, not a working tool yet — a gripper near that rating is genuinely hard to close.
- **Removed:** standing calf raise (Tuesday), band overhead press (Friday, → pike push-up), floor press (→ weighted dips), side-lying triceps extension (→ ring triceps extension), Zercher squat (→ band hack squat).
- **Other swaps from stock Goblin's Gym:** nordic curls for the band stiff-legged deadlift, Bulgarian split squats as the unilateral leg move, ring leg curls for the isometric hamstring.

## Order & balance notes

- **Unilateral default is sequential** (all 4 right, then 4 left). Alternating right/left/right/left also works and gives each side ~2 min recovery — either way it's 8 intervals.
- **Heaviest move first each day** — dips on Monday, split squats on Tuesday, pull-ups on Thursday, squats on Friday.
